Privacy Policy

The DDNet client contacts DDNet's servers to fetch the online server list, news and map downloads. Like any web request, this transmits your IP address, which appears only in web server logs that are deleted after a few days and are not shared with outside parties.

The client does not collect, store or share any personal data. It contains no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking beyond identifying which version it is as well as which platform it's running on.

Online play

When you connect to a game server, your IP address and your chosen nickname are transmitted to that server, as technically required for online multiplayer. On official DDNet servers your race finish times are recorded under your chosen nickname and shown on the public leader boards. Official DDNet servers also store your ingame movement data together with your nickname to verify ranks, but no chat messages. Nicknames are freely chosen and do not need to identify you.

Other game servers you connect to are not controlled by us, but run independently. We have no control over how they use your IP address, ingame chat, and ingame movement data
